## Student Portfolio 
The main goals i've set for myself include staying on top of my assignments and my studying time for exams and test so I can keep and good GPA and graduate with a UX degree. I would also want to get an internship and gain as much experience in the UX field as I can. Moeller.(2012) says that students can have mastery goals that focus on learning, improvement, or performance goals which is more tied to grades. I think my goal to have a good GPA is the closest aligned with a performance goal because it's all about the grades I get, while my internship goal is more of a mastery goal since id be building skills. Both of the articles i've read also stated that students who set goals tend to do better than the people who don't, which supports the way i've planned my school year (Chang.,2018;Moeller.,2012). I found that the goals in the articles were more specific than mine. Moeller.(2012) found that strong goals are measurable, time bound, and come with an action plan. Compared to my goal of just "keeping a good gpa" it doesn't have any specifications to it. Chang. (2018) also had students write their goals every week and review their own progress, the goals in which were short term while mine covers my whole degree. What i've concluded from the articles is that my goals match the ideas about motivation they talk about but need more structure to them. Instead of having my goals be broad i could break them down into weekly task with deadlines so itd be easier to follow through on them.
